Prologue · The quiet before
01 / outsideEverybody kept moving.
I was still trying to understand where I went wrong.
“You good?”“I don't know how to explain it.”
02 / the habitI learned to read pauses, changes in tone, and words that never came.
“Just tell me the truth.”“I'm trying.”
03 / what I wantedI wasn't asking people to be perfect. I wanted honesty. Effort. A reason not to keep guessing.
“I wasn't asking for perfect.”
Chapter I · Things that stayed
04 / memorySome things were my fault. Some things were never mine to fix. I kept carrying both until I couldn't tell them apart.
“You can't fix everything for everyone.”
05 / almostSometimes I could see the kind of connection I wanted right in front of me.

Wanting it didn't mean it could happen.
“Maybe the timing was never ours.”
06 / no answerI kept hoping for one clear explanation. Something real to hold instead of a hundred possibilities.
“Can you just explain?”“I don't know what to say.”
Final chapter · Everything gets quieter
07 / the weightThen the days began to blur together. The messages, the memories, the questions I couldn't settle.

Even resting didn't always feel like rest.
“I'm tired of pretending I'm okay.”
08 / the bodyI was so busy trying to make sense of everything in my head that I started noticing how worn out I felt in my body, too.

I couldn't keep pretending that nothing was happening.
“You seem different lately.”“I feel different lately.”
09 / everything, at onceThe future felt far away. People felt far away. Even the person I used to be felt like someone I only half remembered.

I didn't have a beautiful explanation for it.

I was tired.
“I don't have an answer for everything.”
10 / the last messageI wanted someone to notice without making me explain every little piece of it.

But maybe I had to start by admitting it to myself.
“I needed someone to hear me.”
